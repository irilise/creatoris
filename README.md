# Major systems

## Solar System Simulation

I want to simulate orbital dynamics and the bodies of the solar system to a high enough degree of fidelity to allow for immersive and accurate space travel.

I want to bring detail and depth to the logistics of moving matter across planets, using the resources we have in our stellar neighborhood to bring life to planets like mars and venus.

I want create a sandbox where we can imagine what shipping and logistics on a planetary scale looks like.

## Planetary Simulation

Using open access geographical maps of the planets in the solar system, I want to create a grid based simulation that can make a modular but still accurate representation of what energy generation, food generation, and ecological modification could look like on planets. It will become important to be able to simulate tectonics, atmospheric changes and much more. Each planet will need to be a very detailed simulation that you can enter into and out of.

## The Planet - System Bridge

A smooth transition from looking at what you are doing planetarily into how you are moving resource onto and off world is essential to how this turns out. I want you to be able to fully experience the goings on of each planet as linked with the solar system, and the movement of bodies across the solar system as important and impactful on each planet.
